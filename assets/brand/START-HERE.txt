LOGO SYSTEM
The primary horizontal lockup uses an Ember symbol and Ink wordmark.
The reversed lockup uses Ember and Paper. Ink and Paper one-color masters,
the standalone wordmark, symbol, stacked lockup, and app tile are supplied.
All SVG artwork is genuine vector artwork with outlined lettering.
Transparent PNG exports are included. Background-specific logo exports
are intentionally opaque. SVG masters include clear space.
Minimum lockup width: 160 px. Minimum symbol height: 16 px.
Keep 1/4 symbol height clear around the visible artwork.
Preserve proportions, spacing, lowercase lettering, and orientation.
Do not add effects or decorations to the symbol or lettering.
The adaptive favicon.svg follows the system light/dark preference.
PNG favicons use the dark app tile for predictable contrast.

COLOR
Ink #191C22: primary text and dark surface.
Paper #F5F3ED: main light surface.
Ember #F06A43: brand emphasis and action fill; use Ink text on it.
Clay #F2D8CC: supporting warm surface.
Steel #626B75: secondary text on Paper.
Burnt Ember #A9361C: accent text on Paper.
On Ink, use Paper for primary text and #BBC2CA for secondary text.
Ember text on Paper is not an approved ordinary text pairing.
Semantic colors are specified for light surfaces: success #2C6B50,
warning #805B00, error #B23635. Always include a visible status label.
Approved normal-text pairings are recorded in tokens/brand-tokens.json.
Colors are sRGB. Convert and proof separately for print.

TYPE
Space Grotesk 400/500/600/700: identity, headings, and body text.
JetBrains Mono 400/500: commands, metadata, and technical data.
Display 64/68/600. Heading 32/38/600. Body 16/26/400.
Utility 12/18/500. Scale display type for the medium.
Font originals and their SIL Open Font License notices are included.

VOICE
Clear, capable, warm, and direct. Keep personality in headings and layout.
In diagnostics, state the issue, limitation, and useful next step.
Do not imply full parity with hosted CI or claim unmeasured performance.
Hosted CI continues to provide platform coverage and merge enforcement.

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
Bellows runs your existing GitHub Actions workflows locally using act and
Docker. Preview execution plans, diagnose compatibility issues, and catch
failures before pushing.
