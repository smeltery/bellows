01 / Identity

A practical tool with character.

Bellows brings existing GitHub Actions workflows into the local development loop. Its identity is clear, capable, and warm. The three-fold symbol connects the physical bellows with a closer feedback loop.

  • Keep one quarter of the symbol height clear around the visible lockup.
  • Minimum digital sizes: 16 px symbol height and 160 px lockup width.
  • Use the supplied geometry, orientation, spacing, and color variants.
02 / Color

A warm signal. A quiet base.

Use Paper and Ink for most of the composition. Use Ember for one point of emphasis. Choose Ink text on Ember; use Burnt Ember #A9361C for accent text on Paper.
